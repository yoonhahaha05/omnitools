Privacy Policy

Our mathematical and architectural guarantee: zero server data transmission.

1. Client-Side Execution

OmniTools is designed with a strict Privacy by Architecture standard. All tools—including word counting, cryptographic hashing, JSON parsing, Base64 encoding, regex testing, and password generation—execute entirely within your browser's local sandbox via JavaScript. None of the text, credentials, files, or tokens you paste are ever sent over a network connection to our servers.

2. Local Storage Usage

OmniTools uses your browser's local localStorage API solely to preserve your explicit user preferences: theme selection (Dark/Light mode), view layout density (Grid/List mode), and your list of favorite or recently used tools. This data stays strictly on your physical device.

3. Zero Telemetry & No Tracking

We do not track keystrokes, clipboard actions, or processed file names. You can verify this at any time by inspecting the browser Network tab (F12) while performing any utility operation.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies (Google AdSense)

OmniTools uses Google AdSense to serve non-intrusive advertisements to support the development and free hosting of this platform. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other sites across the Internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or through aboutads.info.

5. Questions & Inquiries

If you have any questions, security audits, or requests regarding OmniTools, contact us at support@getomnitools.com.
