Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

1. Client-Side Execution

OmniTools processes data locally. All utilities—including word counting, cryptographic hashing, JSON formatting, Base64 encoding, regex testing, and conversions—execute entirely within your browser via JavaScript. None of the text, credentials, files, or tokens you enter are sent to our servers.

2. Local Storage Usage

OmniTools uses your browser's local localStorage API solely to preserve your explicit user preferences: theme selection (Dark/Light mode), view layout density (Grid/List mode), and your list of favorite or recently opened tools. This data stays on your device.

3. Zero Data Logging

We do not track keystrokes, clipboard contents, or processed file names. You can verify this at any time by inspecting the browser Network tab (F12) while using any tool.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies (Google AdSense)

OmniTools uses Google AdSense to serve non-intrusive advertisements to support free hosting. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other sites across the Internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or through aboutads.info.

5. Contact

If you have any questions or feedback regarding OmniTools, reach out to us at support@getomnitools.com.
