Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. What stays on your device

Your input stays on this device. All 105 tools—including cryptographic hashing, JSON formatting, Base64 encoding, regex testing, subnet calculation, and image manipulation—run in your browser.

Text, source code, credentials, tokens, and files you type or paste are not sent to an OmniTools server and are not stored in a remote database. Ads are separate and are described in section 3. Ads use cookies.

2. Local Storage & Client State

OmniTools utilizes the HTML5 localStorage API solely to store your personal user interface preferences on your local device:

  • Theme Mode: Dark or Light mode state (omnitools_theme).
  • Layout Density: Grid or compact table display preferences.
  • Favorites & History: Pinned tools and recently used tool identifiers.

This data resides strictly on your machine and can be cleared at any time through your browser's storage settings or DevTools.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies (Google AdSense)

To keep OmniTools completely free and maintain our global CDN infrastructure, we serve non-intrusive advertisements provided by Google AdSense.

Google and third-party advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other sites on the Internet:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized advertisements based on browsing activity.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info or Your Online Choices (EU).

4. GDPR Compliance (European Economic Area)

If you are located in the EEA, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you hold specific statutory rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). OmniTools does not keep an account or a profile of you. Advertising cookies load with the page. You can opt out of personalized ads at the links in section 3.

5. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents are entitled to disclosures regarding the collection, sale, and sharing of personal information. OmniTools does not sell or rent personal information entered into our calculators and utilities to any third party.

6. Contact Data Protection Officer

For inquiries, feedback, or verification regarding our privacy architecture, contact our team at support@getomnitools.com or visit our Contact & Support page.
