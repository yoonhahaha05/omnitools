Overview & Algorithmic Mechanics

Gallons used = distance in miles / miles per gallon. Cost = gallons × price per gallon. A round trip doubles the distance before that division. Splitting by passengers divides the cost, not the gallons.

Worked example. 360 miles in a car that gets 30 mpg uses 12 gallons. At $3.49 a gallon the fuel costs $41.88. A round trip is 720 miles, 24 gallons, and $83.76. Three passengers sharing the one-way cost pay $13.96 each. The page uses US gallons and miles. A liter-based car needs the consumption converted first: 30 mpg is about 7.84 L/100 km.

Worked uses

Usage Steps & Workflow

  1. Input driving distance and vehicle MPG.
  2. Enter local gas price per gallon.
  3. Review total and split cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is fuel consumption calculated?

Total Gallons = Distance / MPG. Total Cost = Gallons * Price Per Gallon.

Does this include food, tolls, or the return trip?

It includes fuel only. Turn on the round-trip control to double the distance.

What is 30 mpg in liters per 100 km?

About 7.84 L/100 km. The calculator itself takes miles, mpg, and a price per gallon.
