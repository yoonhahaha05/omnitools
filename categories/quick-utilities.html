Frequently Asked Questions

How secure is the Password Generator?

It uses the browser's cryptographic window.crypto.getRandomValues() standard, ensuring true cryptographically secure pseudorandom entropy.

Where does the Scratchpad store my notes?

Notes are stored in your browser's local window.localStorage sandbox. They remain on your device even if you close the tab.
